I went back to school after Amazon because I wanted to understand the gap between how products are designed and how people live with them. The thesis, "Towards Design of Interaction Systems for Constrained Spaces", looks at what happens when technology built for large, stable environments ends up in small, moving, improvised ones. The field work started with the #vanlife community (CHI 2021), moved through a phenomenological study of placemaking in small spaces (Graphics Interface 2024), and ended with a physical artefact, the Proteus card deck. Supervised by Mark Hancock; funded by the Ontario Graduate Scholarship, QEII-GSST, and the President's Graduate Scholarship.
