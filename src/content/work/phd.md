- The thesis, "Towards Design of Interaction Systems for Constrained Spaces," follows technology into places it was never designed for and asks three things: what did the design assume, what does the setting do to that assumption, and what would a system built for the setting look like?
- Supervised by Mark Hancock. Funded by the Ontario Graduate Scholarship, QEII-GSST, and the President's Graduate Scholarship.
