- The constraint was the interesting part. Purpose-built screening machines were expensive, and every facility already owned a tablet. So the kiosk is a sensor and a frame, and the tablet is the computer.
- The app waits a set time between scans, so a line at the door does not get double-counted, and the alert temperature starts at 37 °C.
- Voice check-in ("Zoey, check me in") is there because nobody at a door in a pandemic wants to touch a shared screen.
- At senior living chains, the front desk is staffed by nonclinical people, and screening cannot slow visitors down.
- Nothing was collected unless a facility turned visitor tracking on, and the reports went only to a registered address.
