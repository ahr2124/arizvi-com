Indian schools assess constantly and measure very little. Bodhika calibrates items with item response theory, norms them on Indian students, and delivers results through dashboards designed so a wellbeing flag reads as part of a whole picture of the child rather than a label. That design question became "Embed, don't isolate", published at ACM Interactive Health 2026. The company is also my field site: a live institution where the research questions come from teachers and parents rather than from the literature.
