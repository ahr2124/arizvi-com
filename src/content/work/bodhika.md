- Indian schools assess constantly and measure very little. Wellbeing is what they measure worst, because a flag on a report card carries stigma in most families.
- The study asked a simple question: what would a parent-facing report look like if the wellbeing signal sat inside a whole picture of the child (strengths, interests, academics, and then wellbeing)?
- Bodhika Labs is what it took to keep asking that question. A research lab that calibrates items properly (IRT, Indian norms), and a partner school where the teachers and parents bring me the research questions.
