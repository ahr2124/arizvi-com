- Indian schools run a lot of assessments but measure very little. Well-being gets measured worst, because a flag on a report card carries stigma in most families.
- The dashboards put the well-being signal inside a whole picture of the child: strengths, interests, academics, and then well-being.
- At the partner school, the teachers and parents bring me the research questions.
