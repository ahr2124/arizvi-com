TIM (Total Incontinence Management) supplies continence products to senior-living communities across the US. Enrolment ran on a paper form: resident name, product, size, a signature from the resident or their family, and a fax.

The app replaces the form with the shortest path I could design for a nurse who is on her feet and interrupted every ninety seconds: pick the resident, confirm the plan, sign on the phone or send the signature request to a family member by text or email. Everything else (product types, sizes, delivery cadence) is decided from the plan so the nurse never has to think about it.

I own product and engineering for the app and the marketing research behind it, including a set of insight decks segmented by care setting, from independent living to memory care.
