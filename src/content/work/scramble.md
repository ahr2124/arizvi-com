- Card games are one of the oldest interfaces there is, and the hardware hasn't changed in a few hundred years. Proteus asks what happens when it does.
- Each card has a small e-ink display and a radio. An app decides what every card shows and when, so the same 52 objects can be a poker deck, a language-learning set, or a game that mirrors a live broadcast.
- The research question is about familiarity: how much can you change about an object before people stop treating it as the thing they know?
- The company exists for two reasons. The answer turned out to be commercially interesting, and the only way to study the deck at scale is to make a lot of them.
