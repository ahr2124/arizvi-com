Scramble began as Proteus, a research artefact at Waterloo. Each card carries a small e-ink display and a Bluetooth radio, and an app decides what every card shows and when, so the same 52 objects can be a poker deck, a language-learning set, or a game that mirrors a live broadcast. The hardware is deliberately commodity so the design question stays where it belongs: what a familiar physical object can do once it is programmable. I invented the platform, wrote the patents, and published the work with Oliver Schneider and Mark Hancock.
